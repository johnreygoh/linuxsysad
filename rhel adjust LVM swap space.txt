Identify your swap path:
swapon --show

# Usually looks like /dev/mapper/rhel-swap or /dev/vg_name/lv_swap

Turn off swap:
sudo swapoff -v /dev/mapper/rhel-swap

Resize the Logical Volume:
To add 2GB (use -L 4G to set it to exactly 4GB instead):
sudo lvresize -L +2G /dev/mapper/rhel-swap

Format the new space:
sudo mkswap /dev/mapper/rhel-swap

Re-enable swap:
sudo swapon -v /dev/mapper/rhel-swap
